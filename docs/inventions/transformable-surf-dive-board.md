# Transformable Surf + Dive Board (SurfNDive)

## Core Idea
Hybrid board convertible between surf and dive modes, incorporating jet drive, articulating fins, ESP32 controls, battery compartments, and 3D printed lattice hulls.

## Key Design Decisions
- Transformable between surf and dive configurations.
- Jet drive propulsion.
- Articulating fins.
- ESP32 electronics.
- Lattice hulls via 3D printing (Fusion 360 / FreeCAD / Ender 3).
- Builds for transformability and multi-use.

## Materials and Parts
- ESP32.
- Jet drive.
- Batteries.
- 3D printed lattice hull sections.
- Transformation hardware (specifics uncertain).

## Open Questions
